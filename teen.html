START WITH WHAT INTERESTS YOU

A first step into
the world of work.

Funngro calls its young contributors “Teenlancers”. The platform presents project opportunities from brands and businesses, with tasks that can range from content and promotion to feedback and research.

You do not need to pretend to be an expert. Start by understanding the brief, checking whether the task suits you, and being clear about what you can deliver.

READ BEFORE YOU APPLY

Good work starts
with clear terms.

Take a moment to understand the project before accepting. Funngro’s Terms & Conditions set expectations for applicants and state that a job is not guaranteed to every student.

Read the official Terms & Conditions ↗
01

Check the full brief

Understand the scope, deadline, eligibility and what counts as completion.

02

Confirm payment terms

Check the remuneration and payment mechanism before you accept. The terms require clear remuneration details in a job offer.

03

Protect your information

Read the Privacy Policy. For minors, Funngro says parents should supervise the information shared and provide approvals where required.

04

Never pay to unlock a job

Funngro’s terms prohibit training or job programs that require applicants to pay a security deposit, admission fee, test fee or documentation fee.

Visit the official FAQ ↗
Does applying guarantee that I will get a project?+

No. Funngro’s Terms & Conditions explicitly state that it does not guarantee a job to a student. Availability and selection depend on the opportunity and its requirements.

What kinds of projects might be available?+

Funngro’s Teen page names brand promotion, sampling, referrals, surveys, influencer opportunities and content tasks. The live options can change.

How do I submit completed work?+

Follow the individual project instructions. Funngro’s Teen page describes uploading proof such as a photo, screenshot or link for verification.

How and when will I be paid?+

Check the remuneration and payment mechanism in the specific job details or offer before accepting. Funngro’s Terms require those details to be stated in the offer letter; this redesign does not promise a payout time.

Can minors use the platform?+

Funngro’s Privacy Policy discusses minors and says parents should supervise the information shared, with parental approvals required for certain features. Review the latest official eligibility, consent and privacy requirements before registration.
